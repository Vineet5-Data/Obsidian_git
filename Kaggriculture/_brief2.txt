=== ENGINE FACTS (verified in installed kaggle_environments 1.32.4) ===
- price p(I) = base +/- amp * shape(|I - I0|), I0 = 10000, floor $1. Price
  DECREASES in inventory I.
- Market orders are matched BY SLOT INDEX between the two players, then settled
  in a per-unit lockstep loop: unit k of a SELL is priced against inventory
  already raised by units 1..k-1. Within a matched slot both players are quoted
  off the SAME pre-commit inventory (simultaneous, no first-mover edge inside a
  turn). Racing is ACROSS turns only.
- Max 10 market orders per player per turn.
- Exogenous demand DRAINS inventory: 8 shops fire every 4 steps; town centre
  every 12 steps with multiplier 1x/2x/4x from days 0/10/20. Town centre

- Measured glut (2-player supply / seasonal drain): WHEAT 1.00, WOOL 1.20,

  (0 shops AND excluded from town centre => demand exactly zero).
- Labour: HIRE cost follows a Fibonacci schedule (each additional hire costs
  strictly more). Land and animals also purchasable.
- Rival unit positions and the shared market inventory are PUBLIC. Rival's shed

=== WHAT I HAVE ALREADY ESTABLISHED (verify or REFUTE; do not just restate) ===
A) "Sell immediately" dominates: glut >= 1 for every good means every price path
   declines, so withholding always loses. ~75 tested reserve-price / inventory-
   holding policies ALL lost money, monotonically in how much they held.
B) NEW AND IMPORTANT: while WHEN to sell is state-independent, HOW SPREAD OUT
   the selling is matters a lot. Bunching many SELLs into one turn walks the
   price down within that turn (per-unit lockstep); spreading the same volume
   across adjacent turns lets shop/town drain refill demand in between. I
   implemented "advance a sell into a spare slot in an earlier turn whenever the
   shed verifiably holds the goods" and it gained +572 mean margin across a
   288-game panel (win rate 56.2% -> 62.5%) and improved ALL 7 market-parameter
   stress regimes.
C) An evolutionary search over the market channel (48 children, single edits)
   found ZERO improvements: 42% of edits had exactly no effect, the rest ran to
   -35,572. Diagnosis: the action tape is CASH-SATURATED -- adding a purchase is
   silently rejected for want of money, removing one cascades.
D) Perturbing the herd mix away from the recorded 8 COW / 6 SHEEP is strictly
   worse. A from-scratch greedy production controller scores far worse than the
   recorded tape.
